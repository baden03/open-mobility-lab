# Suspension

[Deutsch](suspension_DE.md) · [Engineering](README.md)

**Status:** Requirements discovery

## Objectives

Maintain tyre contact, steering predictability, comfort, and frame protection across expected surfaces and payloads, including asymmetric loads and transformed geometries.

## Questions

- Independent, linked, leaning, or rigid architecture?
- Required travel, rates, damping, anti-roll behaviour, and ground clearance?
- How do bump steer, brake dive, roll centre, unsprung mass, and wheel load transfer behave?
- Does suspension movement interfere with propulsion, access, steering, bodywork, or variable track locks?
- Can settings remain safe across rider and cargo masses?

## Planned evidence

Surface and load profiles, kinematic model, ride/stability simulation, bench characterization, durability tests, and instrumented road tests. See [tadpole layout](../concepts/tadpole-layout.md) and [variable track](../concepts/variable-track.md).
