# Biomechanics

[Deutsch](biomechanics_DE.md) · [Engineering](README.md)

**Status:** Research outline — not medical advice

## Purpose

Define comfortable, efficient, and safe human interfaces for a wide range of bodies and abilities without assuming that maximum power is the primary goal.

## Measurements and models

- Posture, joint angles, reach envelopes, range of motion, grip, and contact pressure.
- Force and power across the stroke, cadence, symmetry, fatigue, and recovery.
- Adjustment ranges and incompatibilities between propulsion, steering, and braking.
- Transfer tasks and consequences of spasm, pain, weakness, or sudden release.

## Protocol principles

Begin with low resistance and adjustable fixtures. Obtain informed consent, minimize personal data, define stop criteria, and involve qualified clinical or ergonomics expertise where appropriate. Record subjective comfort alongside measured output.
