# Dual human propulsion

[Deutsch](dual-human-propulsion_DE.md) · [Concepts](README.md)

**Status:** Exploratory concept

## Hypothesis

Independent foot and hand inputs could let a rider contribute with either or both, vary muscle loading, and continue after fatigue or reduced function in one limb group.

## Architecture questions

- Combine inputs mechanically at a jackshaft, electrically through generation, or through separate driven wheels?
- How do freewheels prevent one input from back-driving the other?
- Can cadence and gearing be tuned independently?
- How are steering, braking, and hand propulsion combined without excessive cognitive or physical load?
- What efficiency, mass, noise, and maintenance penalties arise?

## Validation

Measure usable power, fatigue, joint loading, coordination, fault response, and subjective comfort. Compare against foot-only and hand-only baselines. Coordinate with [biomechanics](../engineering/biomechanics.md) and the [rowing hand drive](rowing-hand-drive.md).
