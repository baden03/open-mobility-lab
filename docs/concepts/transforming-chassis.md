# Transforming chassis

[Deutsch](transforming-chassis_DE.md) · [Concepts](README.md)

**Status:** Exploratory concept

For the integrated three-mode jack-knife proposal, see the [reference vehicle](reference-vehicle.md#4-jack-knife-transforming-chassis).

## Intent

Explore whether one chassis can change geometry or operating mode to offer compact access and parking, low-speed stability, and efficient travel.

## Candidate transformations

- Ride height, seat position, wheelbase, or track width.
- Access mode versus travel mode.
- Manual, assisted, or automatic actuation with a mechanical safe state.

## Required investigation

- Loads, locking integrity, pinch and crush hazards, stability envelopes, and transition conditions.
- Whether transformation may occur while occupied or moving.
- Mass, stiffness, maintenance, and legal-class consequences.
- Interfaces with [variable track](variable-track.md), [suspension](../engineering/suspension.md), and [accessibility](accessibility.md).

## Open questions

- Which user need justifies the added complexity?
- What happens after loss of electrical power or actuator failure?
- How is an incomplete or asymmetric transformation detected and contained?
