# Rowing-inspired hand drive

[Deutsch](rowing-hand-drive_DE.md) · [Concepts](README.md)

**Status:** Exploratory mechanism

For its role in the complete drivetrain and the conservative legal boundary, see the [reference vehicle](reference-vehicle.md#72-rowing-inspired-hand-drive).

## Concept

Convert a comfortable reciprocating pull/push motion into continuous rotary drive. Candidate mechanisms include paired levers, cables or chains, overrunning clutches, a jackshaft, and selectable or continuously variable gearing.

```text
left lever  ── one-way clutch ──┐
right lever ── one-way clutch ──┼── jackshaft ── transmission ── driven wheel
foot crank  ── freewheel ───────┘
```

This diagram is a hypothesis, not a selected design.

## Design questions

- Pull-only, push-pull, alternating, or synchronous operation?
- Lever path, grip rotation, stroke length, force curve, and return assistance?
- Fixed ratio, gears, cam, or variable-radius pulley for useful mechanical advantage?
- Independent left/right operation and safe release under load?
- How can hands remain available for steering and braking?
- How are entrapment, unexpected return, overextension, and repetitive strain prevented?

## Validation

Use adjustable low-load rigs before vehicle tests. Measure forces, motion, efficiency, control interference, fatigue, and joint comfort with [biomechanics](../engineering/biomechanics.md). Review the legal interpretation of indirect human input in [Germany](../regulatory/germany.md).
