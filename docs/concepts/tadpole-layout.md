# Tadpole layout

[Deutsch](tadpole-layout_DE.md) · [Concepts](README.md)

**Status:** Candidate architecture

## Hypothesis

A two-front, one-rear wheel arrangement may offer stable braking, useful steering geometry, and a clear rear drive path while keeping a recumbent or semi-recumbent rider supported.

## Investigate

- Static and dynamic rollover thresholds across load cases.
- Ackermann geometry, scrub radius, bump steer, turning circle, and tyre clearance.
- Front brake balance and behaviour after single-channel failure.
- Visibility, width, kerb interaction, cargo, access, and transport.
- Comparison with delta tricycles, four-wheel layouts, and two-wheel modes.

## Dependencies

See [variable track](variable-track.md), [suspension](../engineering/suspension.md), [accessibility](accessibility.md), and [Germany regulation](../regulatory/germany.md).

Pedro Neves’s [open-source tilting tadpole cargo trike and mid-drive work](open-source-mid-drive-pedro-neves.md) provides a useful packaging and fabrication precedent. It is evidence of a built, rideable development platform—not validation of the reference vehicle’s stability, loads, suspension, or drivetrain.
