# Variable track

[Deutsch](variable-track_DE.md) · [Concepts](README.md)

**Status:** High-risk exploratory concept

## Hypothesis

A wide track may improve stationary and low-speed stability, while a narrow mode may improve access, storage, or path compatibility. The benefit must be demonstrated against the mass and hazards of transformation.

## Safety questions

- Can width change only while stopped, or also in motion?
- What mechanically carries wheel loads and locks the track?
- How are steering geometry, alignment, braking, suspension, and body clearance preserved?
- What prevents asymmetric motion, unintended unlocking, pinch injuries, or a false “locked” indication?
- Which width governs legal classification and permitted infrastructure?

## Evidence needed

Stability simulation, structural load cases, tolerance analysis, failure-mode analysis, lock-cycle tests, and full-load dynamic tests. Coordinate with [transforming chassis](transforming-chassis.md), [tadpole layout](tadpole-layout.md), and [suspension](../engineering/suspension.md).
