# Accessibility

[Deutsch](accessibility_DE.md) · [Concepts](README.md)

**Status:** Requirements discovery

## Scope

Accessibility covers the complete journey: approach, unlocking, loading, entry, adjustment, propulsion, steering, signalling, braking, stopping, parking, charging, maintenance, and exit.

## Research areas

- Transfer height, openings, handholds, clearances, posture, restraint, and pressure distribution.
- Control reach, required force, range of motion, tremor tolerance, and one-sided operation.
- Walking aids, luggage, weather protection, visibility, and personal assistance.
- Emergency stop and exit when power or balance is lost.
- Adjustment without tools and prevention of unsafe settings.

## Method

Co-design with diverse riders. Record functional needs with consent and minimize personal data. Use representative mock-ups and task observation; do not infer capability from age or diagnosis.
