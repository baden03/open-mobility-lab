# Vision

[Deutsch](vision_DE.md) · [Documentation](README.md)

**Status:** Draft

Enable people with changing strength, balance, endurance, or mobility to keep making meaningful journeys under their own agency. The platform should feel like a capable vehicle rather than a clinical appliance, while treating accessibility and safety as first-order engineering requirements.

## Intended outcomes

- Useful everyday range, including journeys beyond walking distance.
- Meaningful rider contribution through whichever limbs and motions are comfortable.
- Assistance that complements rather than erases human propulsion.
- Stable low-speed behaviour and dignified access.
- Configurations that can evolve with the rider.
- Open documentation that others can inspect, challenge, and improve.

## Boundaries

The project does not yet promise a particular range, speed, legal class, geometry, or medical suitability. Those outcomes must follow from user research, regulation, risk analysis, and validation.

## Success questions

- Can a rider enter, operate, stop, and exit safely and independently?
- Can the vehicle remain useful as the rider’s abilities or journey change?
- Is the machine maintainable with available parts and skills?
- Can each intended configuration be operated lawfully where it is used?
