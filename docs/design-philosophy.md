# Design philosophy

[Deutsch](design-philosophy_DE.md) · [Documentation](README.md)

**Status:** Draft principles

## Principles

1. **Design with riders, not around diagnoses.** Measure tasks, preferences, discomfort, and capability directly.
2. **Capability before appearance, dignity throughout.** Accessibility is part of architecture, not a late attachment.
3. **Modularity with controlled interfaces.** Swappable systems require documented mechanical, electrical, and software boundaries.
4. **Graceful degradation.** A single failure should not create an uncontrolled vehicle where practical alternatives exist.
5. **Mechanical clarity.** Prefer mechanisms that can be inspected, understood, and maintained.
6. **Evidence before certainty.** Mark hypotheses and validate them with analysis and tests.
7. **Configuration is part of the design.** A transformed vehicle may enter a different safety or legal envelope.
8. **Bilingual by default.** English and German pages should carry equivalent meaning, not merely literal translation.

## Decision record rule

When a choice excludes a plausible alternative, record the need, options, evidence, decision, date, and consequences in the relevant page or a future decision record.
